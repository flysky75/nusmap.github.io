A LITTLE LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

Your way around NUS.

An independent campus explorer inspired by NTU Map, adapted for the National University of Singapore.

Place names and coordinates come from the NUS campus directory, retrieved 25 September 2026. The directory may contain older listings. Verify room access, outlets and services with NUS.

Map © OpenStreetMap contributors, rendered by Streets GL. Building heights are shown where available; this is not an indoor floor-plan map.

This separate energy-map copy displays NUS building footprints within OpenStreetMap university boundaries. Building sections and tree locations use OpenStreetMap data. Streets GL renders buildings, roads, trees, and terrain from OpenStreetMap and Esri data. Architectural textures are illustrative rather than surveyed photographs. Building and tree heights are estimated where missing. Individual trees in mapped woods and tree rows use schematic placement; they are not a surveyed tree inventory. Energy totals are simulated demo values for September 2026, not actual NUS meter readings.

Directions open in Google Maps. Live shuttle tracking is available through NUS transport information. Saved places stay on this browser.
